Our mission

Every restaurant deserves a menu that keeps up with them.

Paper menus go stale the moment prices change or an item sells out. OpenQRMenu exists so restaurant owners can update their menu in seconds and have every table see it instantly — without touching a printer.

Built for real restaurants, not just tech-forward chains.

We built OpenQRMenu because most digital menu tools were either too complicated, too expensive, or too limited. Restaurant owners shouldn't need a design degree or a developer to publish a menu that looks premium and works flawlessly on every phone.

That's why OpenQRMenu is one flat membership — no confusing tiers, no per-QR fees, and no feature you have to "upgrade" to unlock. Every account gets the full platform: unlimited QR codes, unlimited menus, and enterprise-grade templates.

What we believe

  • Menus should update in seconds, not days.
  • Great design shouldn't be reserved for big chains.
  • Pricing should be simple enough to explain in one sentence.
  • Customers shouldn't need to download anything to see what you're serving.
