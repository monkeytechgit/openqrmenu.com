Platform features

Everything you need to run a modern menu.

From QR generation to live editing and analytics — OpenQRMenu handles the entire digital menu experience, for one flat monthly price.

▦

Unlimited QR codes

Generate a dedicated QR code for your storefront, and one for every table in the house. Print once, and every code stays linked to your live menu — no reprints needed when you make a change.

▤

Unlimited menus & items

Build separate menus for breakfast, lunch, dinner, drinks, or seasonal specials. Add categories, items, photos, prices, and promo pricing without limits.

✦

Enterprise-grade templates

Pick from Minimal, Elegant, or Bold layouts, each designed to make your dishes look their best — from fast-casual to fine dining. Switch anytime, no redesign needed.

◑

Full brand customization

Set your own primary color, background, and typography from a curated set of premium fonts, so your digital menu feels unmistakably like your restaurant.

⚡

Live editing, instantly

Change a price, mark an item sold out, or publish a new seasonal menu — updates go live immediately across every QR code, with zero reprinting.

▲

Scan & view analytics

Track menu views, QR scans, and your best-performing dishes right from your dashboard, so you know exactly what's working.

⛭

No app required

Your customers just scan and view — no app download, no account creation. Works instantly on any smartphone camera.

◈

Team & multi-location ready

Invite your team and manage multiple restaurant locations from a single organization, all under one membership.
