Cafes & coffee shops

Cafes live and die by turnover — guests want to see the menu, order, and get moving. A clean, fast-loading QR menu lets people browse pastries and drink options while they're still in line, and update seasonal drinks the moment they change.

  • Recommended template: Minimal
  • Swap seasonal drinks and pastries in seconds
  • One QR code on the counter, one on every table

Bars & pubs

Cocktail and tap lists change constantly — a new keg goes on, a seasonal cocktail comes off. OpenQRMenu lets bar managers update pricing and availability from a phone, mid-shift, without touching a printer or a laminated card.

  • Recommended template: Bold
  • 86 a draft beer the second the keg kicks
  • Separate happy hour and late-night menus
Bold Preview
🚚

Food trucks

No fixed address, no printed signage that lasts. A single QR code on the truck window works at every stop, every city, every event — and when you change your route menu for a festival, it updates everywhere instantly.

  • Recommended template: Bold
  • One reusable QR code, no reprinting per location
  • Build event-specific menus in minutes
Bold Preview

Fine dining

Upscale guests expect the digital experience to feel as considered as the plating. The Elegant template's dark, serif-driven layout preserves that premium feel, while letting the kitchen update tasting menus and wine pairings course by course.

  • Recommended template: Elegant
  • Nightly tasting menus without new print runs
  • Wine and pairing lists that stay current
Elegant Preview
🥐

Bakeries

Bakeries sell out of items daily, sometimes by mid-morning. Mark a croissant sold out in one tap so guests never walk up to an empty case expecting something the menu promised — and roll out weekend specials without a new sign.

  • Recommended template: Bold
  • Mark items sold out in real time
  • Rotate weekend and holiday specials easily
Bold Preview
🏨

Hotels & resorts

Room service, poolside bar, and the on-site restaurant often need three different menus under one brand. OpenQRMenu's multi-location and unlimited-menus support keeps every outlet on-brand and current from a single dashboard.

  • Recommended template: Elegant
  • Separate menus for room service, pool, and restaurant
  • One organization, consistent branding everywhere
