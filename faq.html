General

What is OpenQRMenu?

OpenQRMenu is a digital menu platform that lets restaurants replace paper menus with QR codes that link to a live, editable menu. Update prices or items anytime and every scan sees the latest version instantly.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Guests simply scan the QR code with their phone's camera and the menu opens instantly in their browser — no app, no account, no download.

What kind of restaurants is OpenQRMenu for?

Any restaurant, cafe, bar, food truck, bakery, or hotel outlet. See our use cases page for template and workflow recommendations by business type.

Pricing

How much does OpenQRMenu cost?

$9.99 USD per month, per restaurant organization. That includes unlimited QR codes, unlimited menus, every design template, and full brand customization — no extra fees.

Is there a per-QR-code fee?

No. Your membership includes unlimited QR codes for every table and location at no extra cost.

Is there a free trial?

You can explore the OpenQRMenu dashboard and onboarding for free. An active $9.99/month membership is required to publish menus and generate QR codes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there is no long-term contract. Manage or cancel your membership anytime from your billing settings.

Menus & QR codes

Can I update my menu after printing the QR code?

Yes. Every QR code is dynamic and points to your live menu. Update prices, items, or availability in your dashboard and every table sees the change instantly, without reprinting anything.

How many menus and items can I create?

Unlimited. Build separate menus for breakfast, lunch, dinner, drinks, or seasonal specials, with as many categories and items as you need.

Can I change templates later?

Yes, you can switch between Minimal, Elegant, and Bold at any time with one click — your menu content stays exactly the same.

Can I match my restaurant's branding?

Yes. Every template supports custom colors, fonts, and backgrounds so your digital menu feels like an extension of your restaurant's brand.

Account & security

Is my data secure?

Yes. Menu data is stored securely and billing is processed through Stripe, so OpenQRMenu never stores your payment card details directly.

Can I manage multiple restaurant locations?

Yes. You can invite team members and manage multiple locations under a single organization and membership.

What payment methods do you accept?

OpenQRMenu uses Stripe for secure billing, supporting all major credit and debit cards.
