How it works

From signup to scannable menu in four steps.

No developer, no designer, no app to install. Most restaurants have their first menu live and QR codes printed within the hour.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email, name your restaurant organization, and you're in your dashboard in under a minute. No credit card required to start exploring.

2

Build your menu

Add categories, dishes, photos, prices, and descriptions with the live editor. Create separate menus for breakfast, lunch, dinner, or happy hour — all included.

3

Choose your look

Pick Minimal, Elegant, or Bold, then match your brand colors, fonts, and background. Preview exactly what guests will see on their phone.

4

Generate & print QR codes

Create unlimited QR codes for your storefront and every table, download print-ready files, and you're live. Every code updates automatically, forever.

After you launch

Keeping your menu current takes seconds.

Your QR codes never need to be reprinted — every edit you make goes live instantly, across every table and location.

✎

Edit prices anytime

Update a price, rename a dish, or adjust a description — changes appear on every guest's phone the moment you save.

◐

86 an item in one tap

Sold out of the salmon? Toggle it off and it disappears from the live menu instantly — no awkward conversations at the table.

▲

Watch performance

Check your dashboard for menu views, QR scans, and your best-performing items to guide what you feature next.

FAQ

Setup questions

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants build their first menu and print their QR codes within an hour. Larger menus with dozens of items may take a bit longer to enter, but the platform itself has no learning curve.

Do I need design or coding skills?

No. Every template is pre-designed and enterprise-grade out of the box. You only choose colors, fonts, and upload photos — no code, no design software.

What do I need to print my QR codes?

Any printer works. Download the print-ready QR code files from your dashboard as PNG or PDF, then print table tents, stickers, or signage however you prefer.

Can I import my existing menu?

Yes. You can add items manually through the live editor, and our support team can help larger menus get started faster — just reach out from the contact page.
