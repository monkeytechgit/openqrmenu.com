FAQ

Common questions

How much does OpenQRMenu cost?

$9.99 USD per month, per restaurant organization. That single membership unlocks unlimited QR codes, unlimited menus, every design template, and full branding customization — no extra fees.

Do I need to download an app?

No. OpenQRMenu runs entirely in your browser. Sign up, build your menu, and print your QR codes — no app download for you or your customers.

Can I update my menu after printing the QR code?

Yes. Every QR code is dynamic — update prices, items, or availability in your dashboard and every table sees the change instantly, without reprinting anything.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, there's no contract. Manage or cancel your membership anytime from your billing settings.
